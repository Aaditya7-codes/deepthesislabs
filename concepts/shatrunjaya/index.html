Investment approach

Opportunity without losing sight of risk.

01

Special situations

Primary focus on event-driven and situation-specific opportunities in Indian equities where company, capital structure or market context may create an attractive setup.

02

Selective compounders

A secondary allocation to thoroughly researched large- and mid-cap businesses where long-duration compounding can complement opportunistic positions.

03

Downside discipline

Capital protection remains a stated priority, with risk considered alongside upside rather than treated as an afterthought.
